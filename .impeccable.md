## Design Context

### Users
The app is for the owner/operator of the IMPO command center who needs to manage
tmux-backed agent sessions from an iPhone. The user is often checking status,
starting a related session, sending short commands, attaching images, and acting
quickly while away from the desktop.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, utilitarian, precise. The interface should feel like a dependable control
surface for work already in progress, not a marketing product or showcase.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dense mobile command center UI with restrained light and dark themes, clear
session hierarchy, stable touch targets, and native-feeling bottom sheets. Avoid
decorative hero layouts, card-heavy marketing composition, noisy gradients, and
anything that makes repeated operational work slower.

### Design Principles
- Keep primary workflows reachable with one hand and resilient to keyboard,
  safe-area, and orientation changes.
- Preserve information density without letting text, actions, or overlays
  collide.
- Prefer familiar native controls and icon actions over explanatory UI text.
- Keep state visible and trustworthy: session status, starred state, active
  selection, and pending operations must be easy to scan.
- Make bottom sheets and send surfaces feel like tools, not modals that block
  the task.

## Dark-mode reading contrast — 2026-10-27

Keep Solarized backgrounds and all light colors unchanged. Dark primary text and neutral actions use #eee8d5; secondary text uses #c0cdcd, matching Web. Inactive cards retain 0.86 opacity in dark mode so their content stays readable.

## Vision Pro input priority — 2026-10-31

The owner frequently operates this app on Vision Pro and cannot reliably type.
Voice and direct button selection are primary inputs there. Keep Start, Stop,
Send, Clear and service selection reachable without a keyboard, hover or long
press. Show microphone state and the actual speech service clearly. Preserve
the current restrained theme and provide large, separated hit targets. Do not
claim real Vision Pro comfort or microphone validation from browser simulation.

## Vision Pro target sizes — 2026-10-31

The owner prioritizes large visible buttons over density on Vision Pro. Use at
least 64-point targets with 12-point separation, labelled Close/Send controls,
and wrapping labelled card actions. Machine selection must be equally easy to
hit. The primary Start/Stop dictation button spans the composer width: 80 points
high on Vision Pro and 64 on other devices. Keep it in place through connecting,
recording and finishing. Do not automatically open a keyboard over voice input.
